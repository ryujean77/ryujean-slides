03 THE WORLDVIEW

A proven model, expressed in the language of Sasang

A PROVEN MODEL
Learning the structure from precedent

3-1. A precedent for building a world-class wellness destination around constitution BENCHMARK

India's Ayurveda made constitution (dosha) a resort experience and opened a global market. We learn from that

  1. Personalization rooted in constitution — Constitution shapes meals, treatments, and the daily routine.
  2. Residential programs — A purposeful journey over several days, beyond a day visit.
  3. Replicable through people and standards — Certified therapists and facility quality grades (Kerala Ayur Silver, Gold, and Diamond)

Kerala classification: Kerala Tourism ↗.

Chopra ↗ — A case of meditation, yoga, and Ayurveda programs within a resort.

Ananda ↗ — A program combining consultation, meals, therapies, and yoga before, during, and after a stay.

Kamalaya ↗ — Guests select a tailored wellness program alongside their accommodation.

SHA ↗ — Individual stay programs built around a proprietary method.

3-2. The language of Sasang

Korea has Lee Je-ma's Sasang medicine (Donguisusebowon, 1894). We explain constitution wellness in a language of our own.

  1. Four constitutions × organ-strength balance (Jangbu daeso) — An anatomical and physiological language describing the relative strength of the liver, lungs, spleen, and kidneys
  2. Temperament (seongjeong) — the constitution of emotion. Constitution includes not only the body but the texture of the mind (sorrow, anger, joy, and pleasure) → extending into meditation, relationships, and leadership programs
  3. Eumyang hwapyeong jiin — the person in yin-yang balance — The balanced person toward whom every constitution aspires. This becomes a statement of brand philosophy.
  4. The specificity of food (sik) — Ingredients, teas, and recipes are already organized by constitution, ready to become F&B offerings.

3-3. Worldview Map — Seven Elements of Life

食 Eat · 眠 Sleep · 動 Move · 香 Breathe · 水 Drink · 衣 Dress · 心 Dwell

→ Every resort touchpoint (rooms, restaurants, spa, gardens, and boutiques) belongs to one of these seven elements. Constitution shapes them all.
