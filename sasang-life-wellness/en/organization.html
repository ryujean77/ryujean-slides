OPERATING PRINCIPLES / 01

Five design principles

01

A small office, strong standards

The office holds the method, training, partner relationships, and data; site teams run day-to-day operations.

02

Education comes before opening

Therapist and consultant training is a Phase 0 prerequisite.

03

Each site is a unit of replication

The VIGO team structure becomes a replication kit for Texas K-Town and partner properties.

04

Automate repetitive tasks from the beginning

Systems handle repetition so people can focus on consultation and care.

05

One standard for claims and safety

Review claims, safety, and personal information practices across every channel.

  1. A small office, strong standards — The office holds the method, training, partner relationships, and data; site teams run day-to-day operations.
  2. Education comes before opening — Therapist and consultant training is a Phase 0 prerequisite.
  3. Each site is a unit of replication — The VIGO team structure becomes a 'replication kit' for Texas K-Town and partner properties.
  4. Automate repetitive tasks from the beginning — Systems handle assessment, reports, booking information, subscriptions, and settlement. People focus on consultation and care.
  5. One standard for claims and safety — Compliance staff review efficacy claims, safety rules, and personal information practices across all channels.

3-3. Five-step onboarding

  1. 1. Proposal and fit review (method lab + compliance) → 2. Constitution mapping (which constitution / therapy and how) → 3. Staff training and on-site trial (four weeks) → 4. Product registration (rooms, spa, boutique, online) → 5. Formal onboarding and quarterly review

3-4. Operating rhythm

  • Monthly Partner Council: Share supply, quality, and guest feedback; plan new seasonal products (24 solar terms)
  • Quarterly Scorecard: Guest satisfaction, repeat purchase rate, on-time supply rate, complaints, and zero claims violations
  • Single Expression Guide: Partner copy goes live only after compliance review (no unverified efficacy claims; language focused on relaxation and lifestyle guidance)
  • Settlement: Sales data is aggregated automatically; settlement terms are discussed separately with each partner.

5-3. Division of roles between people and AI

  • What AI does: Summarize assessment results; draft reports, guidance, messages, and content; aggregate settlements; update dashboards
  • What people do: Confirm constitutions, review final reports, deliver care and service, manage partnerships, and approve outputs
  • Principle: A person reviews all health-related wording before it reaches guests.

5-4. Data and privacy

  • Constitution and health-related information is treated as sensitive data and requires separate consent under Korean privacy law; review state-specific U.S. rules for U.S. operations.
  • Store guest consultation data only in private internal systems, separate from public channels.
  • Genetic and gut-microbiome testing is optional and follows the testing provider's consent process.
