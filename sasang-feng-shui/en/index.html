01 · Concept

People
before
houses

Traditional living feng shui centres on the arrangement of a place and the harmony of yin, yang and the five elements. Yet people, too, carry an energy of their own into a space. We read that person through the Sasang constitution.

We read constitution and sensibility through Sasang medicine, a distinctly Korean tradition that began with Lee Je-ma's Dongui Suse Bowon (1894), and we use the generating and controlling cycles of the five elements as a symbolic language for prescribing space. The interior becomes a tool that guides a person toward a state of harmony: a person of balanced yin and yang (陰陽和平之人).

02 · Principle

Empower strengths,
support weaknesses

木Tae-eum · Liver
土So-yang · Spleen
金Tae-yang · Lungs
水So-eum · Kidneys

A constitution's element is the element of its large organ (liver, spleen, lungs, kidneys). Tae-eum: large liver, small lungs. So-yang: large spleen, small kidneys. So-eum: large kidneys, small spleen. Tae-yang: large lungs, small liver. Because the Sasang constitution divides people by these four organs, 火 (the heart) is not addressed.
Generating cycle 木→火→土→金→水 · Controlling cycle 木→土→水→火→金 · The constitution-element correspondence is not the orthodox doctrine of the original Sasang system; it is Dr. Yim DongKoo's interpretive model for carrying the Sasang constitution into space.

1Reduce (瀉) what is strong

A person already brings the energy of the large organ with them. Piling it up in the space as well tips it into excess, so we take some away.

2Supplement (補) what is weak

We fill in the energy of the small organ through material, color and scent to restore balance.

3Empower the strength

We add the energy that generates the native element, so the strength comes through well without overflowing.

4Support without excess

If the supplementing energy controls the native element, keep it subtle. Filling a weakness should never break a strength.

The five elements are a symbolic language of color, form and material, not rules that forbid real materials. Structural, fire, ventilation and accessibility standards come first. The prescription is a starting point; the final judgement is the resident's comfort.

04 · Method

Five steps

  1. Constitution assessment

    A Sasang constitution questionnaire (such as KS-15) and expert consultation, together with preferences for light, sound and touch.

  2. Five-element space audit

    For each room, materials, colors, forms, lighting and scent are classified by element and their proportions recorded.

  3. Prescription design

    Decide what to reduce, supplement and empower, then choose the accent color and scent.

  4. Styling

    Start with small surfaces (objects, scent, artwork) and widen to large ones (floors, walls, furniture).

  5. Living review

    After two and four weeks, record sleep, fatigue, focus and mood, and adjust.

When a family lives together, keep the four elements evenly balanced in the living room and kitchen, leaning slightly toward the majority constitution; bedrooms and studies follow the prescription of the person whose room it is.

Aroma and diffusers

Build around the constitution's reference oil and blend with lavender, tea tree and lemon. We recommend ultrasonic diffusers or reed diffusion over synthetic air fresheners and burning candles, used intermittently: 30 to 60 minutes on, then a rest. In spaces with infants, pregnant women or pets, check oil types and concentrations separately.
