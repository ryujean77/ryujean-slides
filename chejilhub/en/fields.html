BUSINESS FUSION ATLAS

From a constitution lensto an experience people can use.

Each collaboration follows the same five-step method: discover the person and situation, interpret a hypothesis, curate the industry touchpoint, prototype safely, then measure and update.

01 · DISCOVER

Person and situation

Goal, current state, environment and contraindications.

02 · INTERPRET

Response hypothesis

Describe possibilities and uncertainty, not a fixed verdict.

03 · CURATE

Industry touchpoint

Choose the key scene: lesson, product, stage, meal or space.

04 · EXPERIENCE

Prototype

Run the program with safety, consent and clear language.

05 · EVOLVE

Measure and update

Track understanding, satisfaction, behavior and safety.
