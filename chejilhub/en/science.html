REPRESENTATIVE PAPERS

Two author-participated peer-reviewed studies

Ryu JeongKun’s questionnaire-development study and Dr. DongKoo Yim’s BMI·food-preference study are shown with equal importance.

Ryu JeongKun · 2022

Machine Learning Applications for the Development of a Questionnaire to Identify Sasang Constitution Typology

Kim, Ryu and Park explored item selection, hierarchical clustering and multiple correspondence analysis, identifying 47 core items and six clusters. This is a foundation for improving questionnaire reliability, not an automated diagnosis.

PubMed
Yim DongKoo · 2018

Analysis of BMI and Food Preferences by Sasang Constitution Classified by SCAT2 and Experts

Cheon, Yim and Kim compared SCAT2 with expert classification and examined group-level BMI and food-preference patterns. Classification agreement and food preference are not a personal prescription.

EXTERNAL PAPERS

External evidence, kept separate

Genomics, microbiome, nitric oxide physiology and AI/wearables provide methods and questions—not proof that Sasang-specific effects are established.

Genomics

Explore gene–environment association without assigning constitution or destiny to one SNP. Report cohort size, confounders, effect size and uncertainty.

Microbiome · gut–brain axis

Track diet, sleep, stress and repeated samples over time. Do not convert a small comparison study into a personal treatment claim.

Nitric oxide · vascular response

NO is an important physiological molecule, but constitution-specific effects require preregistered studies, IRB review and safety endpoints.

AI · wearables

Use probability, explanation and uncertainty to support expert judgment and personal choice—not to replace them.
