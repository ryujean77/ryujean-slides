What is Sasang Constitution?

Sasang Constitution is a Korean people-centered tradition systematized by Lee Je-ma in the late nineteenth century. In this hub it is treated as a starting coordinate for self-observation, not a fixed label or destiny.

A LIVING ORIGIN

Difference becomes a choice.

The four constitutional names describe tendencies in physiology and response. Current state, environment, preference, goals and safety must be read alongside the origin model.

Interpretation rule
Constitution is one lens among several. It does not replace medical diagnosis, genetics, or professional care.
